Our Story我们的故事

01 / Brand

In 2018, we ran our first ride out of Guangzhou. China's global era was at full pace, and more and more people were moving across this vast market — for cross-border meetings, client visits, signings, layovers. They didn't just need a car. They needed someone bilingual who could carry them from airport to destination, without a single missed beat.

Years on, our partner network has grown from a focused beginning into a hospitality network across Greater China. Whether it's the first-time arrival of an international executive or the third weekly trip of a regular client, the standard from that very first ride — punctual, quiet, proper — remains the only standard we know.

We are 光年. Light is silent and exact. Year is daily commitment. We arrive the way light does. We hold the way years do.

2018 年，我们在广州开出第一程。彼时中国的全球化正酣，越来越多人在这片广袤的市场穿梭奔波 —— 跨境会议、客户拜访、签约转机。他们需要的不只是一辆车，而是一个能用双语、从机场到目的地全程托底的人。

数年过去，我们的合作网络从最初的专注起步，扩展为横跨大中华区的礼宾出行网络。无论是国际高管的首次抵达，还是常旅客户每周第三次接送，第一程的标准 —— 准时、安静、得体 —— 仍是我们唯一的标准。

我们叫「光年」。光，是无声而精准；年，是日复一日的持守。我们用光的方式抵达，用年的尺度坚守。

— LyctRides · Guangzhou · Since 2018—— 光年 · 广州 · 自 2018
