Privacy Policy隐私政策

Last updated: 14 June 2026 · v1.0最近更新：2026 年 6 月 14 日 · v1.0

This Privacy Policy explains how LyctRides Car Service Co., Ltd. ("LyctRides", "we", "us") — the operator of the LyctRides brand and the website lyctai.com — collects, uses, stores and protects personal information when we provide corporate car service, business ground transportation and related coordination, fulfilment-management and reconciliation services to our enterprise clients. We follow the principle of minimum necessity under the Personal Information Protection Law of the PRC (PIPL). 本隐私政策说明 光年（广州）汽车服务有限责任公司（以下简称"光年""LyctRides""我们"，LyctRides 品牌及 lyctai.com 网站的运营方）在向企业客户提供企业用车、商务出行及相关行程协调、履约管理、对账等服务过程中，如何收集、使用、存储和保护个人信息。我们严格遵循《中华人民共和国个人信息保护法》（PIPL）的最小必要原则。

Our services are provided on a business-to-business (B2B) basis to enterprise clients. At this stage we do not offer ride-hailing or instant on-street dispatch to the general public, and we do not collect more information than is necessary to coordinate and fulfil a booked itinerary. 我们的服务以企业对企业（B2B）方式提供给企业客户。现阶段我们不面向不特定公众提供即时叫车服务，亦不会收集超出协调与履约一段已预约行程所必需范围的信息。

1 · Who is responsible for your information一 · 个人信息处理者

The personal information handler is LyctRides Car Service Co., Ltd., registered in the People's Republic of China. Registered address: No. 2 Haijing Road, Panyu District, Guangzhou. For privacy enquiries, contact bd@lyctai.com. 个人信息处理者为光年（广州）汽车服务有限责任公司，注册地址为广州市番禺区海景路2号。隐私相关事宜请联系 bd@lyctai.com。

2 · Information we collect二 · 我们收集的信息

We collect only what is necessary to deliver the service:我们仅收集提供服务所必需的信息：

  • Client contact details — the name, job title, company, email and phone number of the enterprise contacts and bookers who liaise with us.客户对接信息 —— 与我们对接的企业联系人及预订人的姓名、职务、所属企业、邮箱与电话。
  • Itinerary information — pickup and drop-off locations, dates and times, passenger names and contact numbers, and special requirements, where needed to coordinate and fulfil a booking.行程信息 —— 用车的上下车地点、日期与时间、乘车人姓名与联系电话、特殊需求等，仅在协调与履约预约所必需时收集。
  • Billing and reconciliation information — cost-centre tags, invoicing entity and billing records associated with corporate accounts.账单与对账信息 —— 与企业账户关联的成本中心标签、开票主体与账单记录。
  • Communications — correspondence with our team via email, WeChat, WhatsApp, phone or the website enquiry form.沟通记录 —— 通过邮件、微信、WhatsApp、电话或网站表单与我们团队的往来沟通。
  • Technical information — basic website logs (e.g. IP address, device/browser type) needed to keep the site secure and functioning. We do not use the website for advertising profiling.技术信息 —— 维持网站安全与正常运行所需的基础访问日志（如 IP 地址、设备/浏览器类型）。我们不将网站用于广告画像。

3 · How and why we use information三 · 使用目的与依据

We use the information above to:我们将上述信息用于：

  • coordinate, manage and fulfil booked itineraries, including dispatching the requirement to a qualified supplier;协调、管理并履约已预约的行程，包括将用车需求转交持牌合作供应商；
  • communicate with you about bookings, changes and service support;就预订、变更及服务支持与您沟通；
  • prepare invoices, statements and reconciliation for corporate accounts;为企业账户出具发票、账单并完成对账；
  • maintain service quality, security and recordkeeping; and维护服务质量、安全与必要的记录留存；以及
  • comply with applicable laws and regulations.遵守适用的法律法规。

Our legal bases include the necessity of performing a contract with the enterprise client, our reasonable operating needs, and — where required — your consent.我们处理个人信息的依据包括：为与企业客户订立、履行合同所必需，合理的经营需要，以及在法律要求时取得的同意。

4 · Sharing with fulfilment suppliers四 · 与履约供应商的共享

Vehicles and driving are provided by qualified partner suppliers that hold the relevant operating qualifications; those qualifications belong to the suppliers. To fulfil a booking, we share with the assigned supplier only the minimum itinerary information needed for that trip (such as pickup/drop-off, time, and the passenger's name and contact number). We bind suppliers to confidentiality and data-protection obligations. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for third-party marketing. 车辆与驾驶服务由具备相应经营资质的合作供应商提供，相关资质归供应商所有。为履约一段行程，我们仅向被指派的供应商共享该次行程所必需的最小信息（如上下车地点、时间，以及乘车人姓名与联系电话）。我们要求供应商承担保密与数据保护义务。我们不出售个人信息，亦不为第三方营销目的共享个人信息。

5 · Storage, retention and cross-border五 · 存储、保存期限与跨境

We process and store personal information within the People's Republic of China by default, and retain it only for as long as necessary to provide the service, meet legal/accounting requirements, or resolve disputes. Generally, trip and communications records are retained for no more than 2 years; invoicing and reconciliation records are retained as required by applicable law (typically 5 to 10 years for accounting purposes). Should a cross-border transfer ever be necessary (for example, where an overseas enterprise client requires it), we will do so only on a minimum-necessary basis and with the separate consent and safeguards required by the PIPL. 我们默认在中华人民共和国境内处理和存储个人信息，且仅在提供服务、满足法律/财务要求或处理争议所必需的期限内保存。一般情况下，行程与沟通记录保存不超过 2 年；财税发票与对账记录依据适用法律保存（通常不少于 5 至 10 年）。如确需进行跨境传输（例如境外企业客户有此要求），我们将仅在最小必要范围内进行，并依据《个人信息保护法》取得单独同意并采取相应保障措施。

6 · Security六 · 安全措施

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures — access control, encryption in transit, and limiting access to a need-to-know basis — to protect personal information against unauthorised access, disclosure or loss.我们采取合理的技术与管理措施 —— 访问控制、传输加密、按需最小授权 —— 以保护个人信息免遭未经授权的访问、披露或丢失。

7 · Your rights七 · 您的权利

Subject to applicable law, you may request to access, correct, or delete your personal information, withdraw a consent you previously gave, or obtain an explanation of our processing rules. To exercise these rights, contact bd@lyctai.com. We will respond within the period required by law. You may also lodge a complaint with the competent personal information protection authority.在适用法律允许的范围内，您可以请求查阅、更正或删除您的个人信息，撤回此前作出的同意，或要求我们解释处理规则。如需行使上述权利，请联系 bd@lyctai.com，我们将在法律规定的期限内回复。您也有权向履行个人信息保护职责的主管部门投诉、举报。

8 · Cookies and website data八 · Cookie 与网站数据

The website uses only the storage strictly necessary for basic functions (such as remembering your language preference). It does not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.本网站仅使用维持基础功能所必需的存储（例如记住您的语言偏好），不使用广告或跨站追踪 Cookie。

9 · Minors九 · 未成年人

Our services are directed at enterprise clients and are not intended for individuals under 14. We do not knowingly collect the personal information of children.我们的服务面向企业客户，不面向 14 周岁以下个人。我们不会在知情情况下收集儿童的个人信息。

10 · Updates to this policy十 · 政策更新

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a revised "last updated" date.我们可能不时更新本政策。重大变更将在本页面更新，并标注新的"最近更新"日期。
