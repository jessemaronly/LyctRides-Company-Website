05 / Service

Long-term Charter. 长 包 车

Same chauffeur. Same car. Same standard — month after month. 同一管家，同一辆车，同一标准 — 月月如一。

§ 02 · Inclusions

What's included. 服务包含

  • 01

    Consistent chauffeur相对专属管家

    A consistent chauffeur, assigned by our partner supplier for the term — learns your preferences, your routes, your itinerary patterns. 合约期内为您配备一位相对固定的管家 — 熟悉您的偏好、常用路线与行程规律。

  • 02

    Reserved vehicle固定车辆

    A fixed vehicle reserved exclusively for you — not pooled, not reassigned. Choice of S-Class, V-Class, ZEEKR 009, or GL8. 为您专门预留的固定车辆 — 不参与共享调度。可选 S-Class、V-Class、ZEEKR 009、别克 GL8。

  • 03

    Flexible terms灵活合约

    Annual contracts and longer. Billed monthly or quarterly. Pause for travel periods, scale up for special events. 年度起约（含多年合约），按月或按季度结算。差旅期间可暂停，活动期间可临时扩配。

  • 04

    Cross-city coverage跨城支持

    Your assigned chauffeur and vehicle move with you across Greater China — or we coordinate seamless handoffs to a sister vehicle in the next city. 专属管家与车辆可随您跨城使用 — 或由我们统筹安排在下一个城市的同标准车辆无缝衔接。

§ 03 · Coverage

Where expats live. 外籍长居 · 覆盖城市

Long-term charter is available in every Tier-1 city and most major commercial hubs across Greater China. 长包车服务覆盖所有一线城市及大中华区主要商业枢纽。

  • Shanghai

  • Guangzhou

    Zhujiang New Town, Tianhe广州 · 珠江新城 · 天河

  • Shenzhen

    Futian, Nanshan, Bay Area深圳 · 福田 · 南山 · 大湾区

  • Hong Kong

    Central, Mid-Levels, Kowloon香港 · 中环 · 半山 · 九龙

  • Hangzhou · Suzhou · Chengdu · +

    All Tier-1 commercial hubs杭州 · 苏州 · 成都 · 等一线商业城市

Long-term charter chauffeurs, assigned through our partner suppliers, are English-speaking and briefed to handle expat family logistics — school runs, embassy visits, weekend trips, airport runs — with the same chauffeur each day where possible. 长包车的管家均具备英语沟通能力，熟悉外籍家庭日常需求 — 学校接送、使馆办事、周末出行、机场往返 — 尽量每天由同一位管家服务。
