02 / Service

Hourly & Daily Hire. 时 租 与 日 租

Your chauffeur, your route, your hours. 你的司机，你的路线，你的时间表。

§ 02 · Inclusions

What's included. 服务包含

  • 01

    Reserved chauffeur, half or full day半日或全日预约司机

    Half-day (4 hours), full-day (8 or 10 hours), and multi-day options. Your chauffeur stays with you from start to end. 半日（4 小时）、全日（8 / 10 小时），以及多日预订。同一位司机全程陪伴。

  • 02

    Unlimited stops任意起止点

    Within your reserved hours, stop anywhere — meetings, lunch, retail, gym — no per-stop fees. 预约时段内任意停靠 — 会议、午餐、购物、健身 — 无额外停靠费。

  • 03

    Same chauffeur returns常旅客户专属司机

    Multi-day or recurring clients get the same chauffeur where possible — familiar with your office lobby and preferred routes. 多日预订或常旅客户尽可能配同一位司机 — 熟悉您办公楼大堂与回家路线。

  • 04

    In-cabin amenities车内常备

    Bottled water, WiFi, mobile chargers, umbrellas, child seat on request, and a quiet cabin tuned for calls. 瓶装水、WiFi、移动充电、雨伞，儿童安全座椅按需提供，车内静谧适合通话工作。

  • 05

    Itinerary changes mid-day行程随时调整

    Plans shift? Just tell the chauffeur. No re-booking, no surcharges — we built the service to flex with you. 行程临时变更？直接告诉司机。无需重新预订、无附加费 — 灵活随行。

§ 03 · Process

How it works. 服务流程

Step 01

Choose hours & vehicle.选 择 时 段 与 车 型

Specify pickup time, hours needed (4h / 8h / 10h / multi-day), and vehicle type. 确定接车时间、所需时长（4h / 8h / 10h / 多日）与车型。

Step 02

We assign.调 度 安 排

A vetted bilingual chauffeur is dispatched. Driver info pushed to your phone before pickup. 调度通过认证的双语司机，接车前推送司机信息至您手机。

Step 03

Adjust as you go.随 行 调 整

Tell the chauffeur about itinerary changes directly. We handle re-routing and parking. 行程调整直接告知司机，我们负责改道与停车安排。

Five client meetings across CBD, lunch in between, end-of-day to airport — one chauffeur, one fee. 一日五场客户会议，中途午餐，傍晚送机 — 一位司机，一份费用，全程托底。

Site visit / due diligence尽 调 / 现 场 考 察

Tours of factories, warehouses, retail sites — chauffeur waits and re-routes per your decisions on the ground. 工厂、仓库、门店巡查 — 司机随机应变，按现场决策实时改道。
