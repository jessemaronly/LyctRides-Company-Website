04 / Service

Business & Events. 商 务 与 活 动

From a 200-person summit to a couture week. 从 200 人峰会到时装周。

§ 02 · Inclusions

What's included. 服务包含

  • 01

    Dedicated event coordinator专 属 活 动 协 调

    A single point of contact for the entire program — agenda mapping, daily briefings, on-site coordination, post-event reporting. 全程对接 — 议程梳理、每日简报、现场协调、活动后报告。

  • 02

    Multi-vehicle dispatch车 队 调 度

    Up to 100+ vehicles operating simultaneously across multiple venues. Real-time GPS tracking and centralized dispatch. 同步调度 100+ 辆车跨多场地运营。实时 GPS 追踪 + 中央调度系统。

  • 03

    Driver briefing protocol司 机 简 报 协 议

    Drivers briefed on VIP roster, dress code, NDAs, and event-specific protocols. Uniform attire on request. 司机针对贵宾名单、着装、保密协议、活动流程进行简报。可定制统一着装。

  • 04

    On-site dispatch desk现 场 调 度 台

    Coordinator team stationed at venue or hotel lobby. WeChat / WhatsApp groups for real-time guest movement. 现场协调团队驻扎活动场地或酒店大堂。微信 / WhatsApp 群组实时管理嘉宾动线。

  • 05

    Pre-event scouting路 线 预 勘

    Route scouting, venue access permit coordination, parking allocation, contingency planning before guests arrive. 提前实地勘察路线、协调场地通行证、规划停车与应急方案。

  • 06

    24h debrief & monthly invoicing事 后 复 盘 + 月 度 对 账

    Post-event report within 24 hours. Consolidated invoicing — one PO, one settlement. 活动结束 24 小时内提交复盘报告。集中开票 — 一笔订单、一次结算。

§ 03 · Process

How it works. 服务流程

Step 01

Brief us.需 求 简 报

Event dates, locations, attendee count, VIPs, transportation needs — we propose a fleet plan and on-site team. 活动日期、场地、参会人数、贵宾、用车需求 — 我们提供车队方案与驻场团队建议。

Step 02

We propose.方 案 评 审

Detailed proposal: vehicle counts, driver assignments, route maps, on-site coordinators, contingency plan. 详细方案：车辆数、司机分工、路线图、现场协调员、应急预案。

Step 03

Execute & debrief.执 行 与 复 盘

Run the event with real-time dispatch. Submit post-event report within 24 hours, monthly consolidated invoice. 现场实时调度执行。活动后 24 小时提交复盘报告，月底集中开票。
