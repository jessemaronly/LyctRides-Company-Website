03 / Service

City-to-City Rides. 城 际 通 行

Door-to-door across Greater China — including the Hong Kong / Shenzhen cross-border. 跨城门到门 — 含港深跨境直达。

§ 02 · Inclusions

What's included. 服务包含

  • 01

    Hong Kong ↔ Shenzhen dual-plate港深双牌车 · 一次过关

    Single-stop immigration at 皇岗 (Huanggang) or 深圳湾 (Shenzhen Bay). The vehicle holds both HK and Mainland plates — you stay seated while officers stamp passports through the window. 皇岗或深圳湾口岸一次性过关。车辆同时持港牌与内地牌，旅客无需下车换乘，海关在车内完成验证。

  • 02

    Mainland Travel Permits handled回乡证 / 港澳通行证协调

    For international passengers without HK Mainland Travel Permits, we coordinate Group Visa pre-applications and submit paperwork on your behalf. 国际旅客如无港澳通行证，我们代为提交团签 / 个签材料，提前协调海关流程。

  • 03

    Bilingual cross-border chauffeur双 语 跨 境 司 机

    Trained on Customs and Immigration procedures (HK & Mainland), Mandarin-Cantonese-English fluent — handles edge cases at the border calmly. 熟悉两地海关流程，国粤英三语沟通 — 跨境突发情况冷静应对。

  • 04

    All tolls, fuel, permits included高 速 / 燃 油 / 证 件 全 包

    No surprise charges at toll plazas. Cross-border permits and inspection fees are part of the quote. 高速公路通行费、燃油、跨境证件费全部包含，不收尾段附加费。

  • 05

    Pre-planned route with rest stops路 线 与 休 息 点 预 设

    For 200km+ journeys, planned rest stops at preferred service plazas. Chauffeur briefed on weather and traffic conditions. 200 公里以上行程预设休息点。司机出发前同步天气与路况简报。

§ 03 · Process

How it works. 服务流程

Step 01

Submit travel docs.提 交 出 行 信 息

Route, passenger names, passport / Mainland Travel Permit info, preferred date and time. 48-hour notice for cross-border. 提交路线、旅客姓名、护照 / 港澳通行证信息、出行日期。跨境业务需提前 48 小时申请。

Step 02

We coordinate.路 线 与 海 关 协 调

Dual-plate vehicle assigned, customs paperwork pre-submitted, chauffeur briefed. 调度双牌车辆，预提交海关申报材料，司机简报。

Step 03

Single-stop transit.一 次 过 境

From pickup point to destination — door-to-door, single stop at the border, total around 90 minutes for HK ↔ Shenzhen. 从接车地点到目的地 — 门到门、口岸一次停留，港深跨境全程约 90 分钟。
