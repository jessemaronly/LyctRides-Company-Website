01 / Service

Airport Transfers. 机 场 接 送

From the gate to the meeting — without missing a beat. 从舱门到会议桌，分秒不差。

§ 02 · Inclusions

What's included. 服务包含

  • 01

    Real-time flight tracking实时航班追踪

    Auto-adjust for delays, gate changes, and rerouting. The chauffeur's ETA shifts as your flight does — no re-booking, no follow-up calls. 航班延误、换登机口、改道实时同步。司机的抵达时间随航班调整 — 无需重新预订或电话跟进。

  • 02

    Name-board meet & greet到达大厅举牌接机

    At all major airports across China — PEK, PVG, CAN, SZX, HKG, MFM, TPE and beyond. Custom name-boards prepared for VIP arrivals. 覆盖 PEK / PVG / CAN / SZX / HKG / MFM / TPE 等主要国际机场，VIP 客户可定制举牌设计。

  • 03

    Generous post-landing wait到港后充裕等候

    Chauffeur stays through immigration, baggage, and customs. No clock-watching, no surprise charges. 司机全程等候通关、提取行李、海关过检。不计较时间，不收意外费用。

  • 04

    Pre-arrival WeChat / WhatsApp push抵港前微信 / WhatsApp 推送

    Driver name, photo, and vehicle plate sent before you land. Find your ride in seconds at the curb. 落地前收到司机姓名、头像、车牌信息，在到达大厅几秒内找到接车。

  • 05

    In-cabin essentials车内常备

    Bottled water, WiFi, mobile chargers, umbrellas. Child seats and dietary preferences on request. 瓶装水、WiFi、移动充电、雨伞。儿童安全座椅与饮食偏好可按需提供。

§ 03 · Process

How it works. 服务流程

Step 01

Submit details.提 交 信 息

Flight number, passenger name, and pickup destination. Submit by WeChat, email, or our concierge desk. 航班号、旅客姓名、接机目的地。可通过微信、邮件或礼宾台提交。

Step 02

We dispatch.车 辆 调 度

Bilingual chauffeur briefed with passenger context. Vehicle dispatched, name-board prepared. 双语司机针对旅客信息进行简报，车辆调度，举牌准备完成。

Step 03

Greet & depart.接 机 出 发

Greet at arrivals, luggage to vehicle, depart for destination. Pre-arrival driver info already on your phone. 到达大厅举牌接车，行李上车，直送目的地。司机信息抵港前已推送到您手机。
