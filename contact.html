How is a single booking different from a long-term charter? 「单次预约」和「长包车」有什么区别？
A single booking is a one-off trip — single airport transfer, door-to-door city trip, or short-term booking, billed per trip with no commitment. A long-term charter is a contract of one year or more under which our partner supplier assigns a consistent driver and vehicle to you, typically billed monthly or quarterly. Ideal for executives with recurring daily needs. 单次用车即单次预约出行——单次机场接送、市内门到门或短期预订，按趟计费、无需长期承诺。长包车则是一年起签的合约，为您指派相对固定的管家与车辆，通常按月或按季度结算，适合有日常高频出行需求的高管群体。
