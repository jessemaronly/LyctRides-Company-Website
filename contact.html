Do you serve individual travellers? 你们也接受个人预订吗？
Yes. While our platform is optimised for corporate travel, individual travellers and families are equally welcome. Single airport transfers, on-demand orders (零单), and long-term charter (长包车) are all available for personal use. 当然。我们的平台虽主要面向企业差旅，但同样欢迎个人及家庭预订。机场接送、零单市内用车、长包车均可个人使用。
How is an on-demand order (零单) different from a long-term charter (长包车)? 「零单」和「长包车」有什么区别？
An on-demand order (零单) is a one-off ride — single airport transfer, point-to-point city trip, or short-term booking, billed per trip with no commitment. A long-term charter (长包车) is a contract of one year or more that assigns a dedicated driver and vehicle to you, typically billed monthly or quarterly. Ideal for executives with recurring daily needs. 零单是单次按需出行，按趟预订无需承诺。长包车则是按月或按季度配备专属司机与车辆，适合有日常高频出行需求的高管群体。
