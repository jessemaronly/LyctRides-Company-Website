Why LyctAI 为何存在

Promises happen
in the details. 承诺
在细节里发生

A ground transport company does not fail at the headline. It fails at the missing flight number, the address typed into the wrong field, the WeChat message left half-parsed at 2 AM. 一家地面出行公司不会在大方向上失败 —— 它通常败在没填的航班号、填错位置的地址、凌晨两点没人来得及整理的微信消息上。

LyctAI is how we industrialise the part that used to depend on individual diligence. Language models read what people actually wrote. Business rules turn that into the 22 fields an order requires. A human always confirms the final commitment. LyctAI 把过去依赖个人细心的环节工业化。大模型读取人们实际写下的文字，业务规则把它转化为订单所需的 22 个结构化字段，关键决策永远由人来完成最后一步确认。

It is not a chatbot. It is a discipline. 这不是聊天机器人，这是一种工程纪律。

Technology Foundation 技术底层

Four layers, one discipline. 四层架构，一种纪律

The intelligence is not in any single model — it is in the way we put models, rules, and humans in the right order. 智能不在某一个模型里 —— 而在我们把模型、规则、人按正确的顺序组合起来的方式上。

01 · MODEL LAYER

Domestic Models First 国产模型优先

DeepSeek and Qwen via OpenAI-compatible APIs. Data stays within mainland infrastructure. Models are swappable per capability. 通过 OpenAI 兼容协议接入 DeepSeek 与通义千问，数据在境内基础设施流转。每个能力可单独切换模型。

02 · GATEWAY LAYER

Unified AI Gateway 统一 AI 网关

A single internal gateway routes every AI call. It handles provider selection, timeout, fallback, and structured JSON parsing — so business code stays clean. 所有 AI 调用走统一内部网关，统一处理路由、超时、降级与结构化 JSON 解析，业务代码保持简洁。

03 · BUSINESS LAYER

Grounded in Rules 扎根于业务规则

Output is normalised against the same DTO validators, RBAC checks, and state machine that govern manual orders. AI cannot bypass what humans cannot bypass. AI 输出统一经过订单 DTO 校验、RBAC 权限与状态机检查 —— 人工不能跳过的环节，AI 同样不能。

04 · SAFETY LAYER

Human Confirms, Always 人工确认，永远

No model output is ever written to the order, billing, or settlement database directly. A human always reviews and confirms before commit. Every call is logged. 没有任何模型输出直接写入订单、计费、结算数据库。任何提交前都由人审核确认，每一次调用都进入审计日志。

Principles 设计原则

What LyctAI will not do. LyctAI 不做什么

01

AI does not replace the promise. AI 不替代承诺

It makes the promise easier to keep — never weaker. 它让承诺更易兑现，从不削弱承诺本身。

02

Human in the loop. 人在决策环上

Every consequential decision passes through a human before commit. 每一个有后果的决策，最终由人确认。

03

Domestic models first. 国产模型优先

Data stays close to where business runs. Compliance is not an afterthought. 数据贴近业务运行的地方，合规不是事后补救。

04

Explainable & auditable. 可解释 可审计

Every AI call carries a version, an actor, and a record. We can show our work. 每一次 AI 调用都带版本号、操作者与记录，可以追溯。
